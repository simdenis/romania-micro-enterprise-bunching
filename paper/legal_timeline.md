**Note A — the 2022→2023 transition.** OG 16/2022 in its original form would have tested the 500,000 EUR ceiling at 31 Dec 2022 (ANAF Brașov guide of 01.11.2022: "Primul an de aplicare al noilor prevederi este anul 2023 pe baza verificării îndeplinirii acestor condiţii la 31 decembrie 2022"). Legea 370/2022 art. I pct. 19 then rewrote OG 16/2022 art. IX lit. e): *"e) prevederile pct. 4, 30, 43, 48, 49, 51 - 53, 71, 73 - 75, 81, 83 - 86 se aplică începând cu veniturile aferente anului 2023;"* — pct. 4 being the 500,000 EUR ceiling (verbatim from ANAF DGRFP Brașov table `legea_370.pdf`, 28.12.2022). Consequently, for the 31 Dec 2022 status test the 1,000,000 EUR ceiling still applied and firms with 2022 revenue between 500k and 1M EUR could remain micro for 2023 (this is also the reading of EY Tax Alert 3/2023 and the Romanian tax press **[secondary]**; I found no MF/ANAF communiqué stating it expressly, so treat the primary text as the authority). The other new conditions (≥ 1 employee, 25% rule, ≤ 20% consulting) were **not** deferred and were tested at 31 Dec 2022. The 500k limit therefore first bit as the in-year exit limit during 2023 (from the quarter it was exceeded, converted at BNR 31 Dec 2022) and at the 31 Dec 2023 test for 2024 status.

**Note B — what "revenue" means.** 2013–2015: norms pct. 4/12 — same elements as the taxable base (art. 112^7). 2016–2025: art. 54 — same elements as the art. 53 taxable base (i.e. excluding, e.g., subsidies, FX gains, from 2021 dividends). From 2026: cifra de afaceri per accounting rules (OUG 8/2026). From 2024 the ceiling is tested on the sum over linked enterprises (> 25% links; from OUG 31/2024 also PFA/II/IF income of > 25% holders).

## 2. Exchange-rate rules

Two different rules apply, and in every version both point to the **BNR rate at the close of the financial year preceding the year of application**:

**(a) Eligibility at 31 December of year t−1 (status for year t):** revenue of year t−1 is converted at the BNR rate valid at the close of year t−1 (the year in which the revenue was recorded).

- 2013–2015 (Legea 571/2003, art. 112^1 lit. c) has no rate sentence; the rule is in the Norme, HG 44/2004, pct. 4 for art. 112^1): *"Pentru încadrarea în condiţia privind nivelul veniturilor realizate în anul precedent, se vor lua în calcul aceleaşi venituri care constituie baza impozabilă prevăzută la art. 112^7 din Codul fiscal, iar cursul de schimb pentru determinarea echivalentului în euro este cel de la închiderea aceluiaşi exerciţiu financiar."* (2014–2015 wording refers to "art. 112^1 lit. a^1) şi lit. c)").
- 2016 onwards (Legea 227/2015, **art. 47 alin. (1) lit. c)**, identical sentence in OUG 50/2015, OUG 3/2017, OUG 79/2017, OG 16/2022, OUG 156/2024, OUG 8/2026): *"c) a realizat venituri care nu au depăşit echivalentul în lei a [100.000 / 500.000 / 1.000.000 / 500.000 / 250.000, respectiv 100.000 începând cu 1 ianuarie 2026 / 100.000] euro. Cursul de schimb pentru determinarea echivalentului în euro este cel valabil la închiderea exerciţiului financiar în care s-au înregistrat veniturile;"*
- Only quirk: the HoReCa mixed-activity rule inserted by Legea 370/2022 (art. 47 (5) lit. c), 2023 only) used *"cursul de schimb ... valabil la închiderea exerciţiului financiar precedent celui în care s-au înregistrat veniturile"*.

**(b) In-year exit during year t:** cumulated revenue from 1 January of year t is compared with the ceiling converted at the BNR rate valid at the close of year t−1.

- 2013 (Legea 571/2003, **art. 112^6**, OG 8/2013): *"Prin excepţie de la prevederile art. 112^2 alin. (5) şi (7), dacă în cursul unui an fiscal o microîntreprindere realizează venituri mai mari de 65.000 euro, aceasta va plăti impozit pe profit luând în calcul veniturile şi cheltuielile realizate de la începutul anului fiscal. Calculul şi plata impozitului pe profit se efectuează începând cu trimestrul în care s-a depăşit limita prevăzută în prezentul articol. ... În cazul contribuabililor prevăzuţi la art. 112^2 alin. (3), limita de 65.000 euro şi impozitul pe profit datorat se calculează în mod corespunzător de la 1 februarie 2013."* Rate: Norme pct. 13 for art. 112^6: *"Cursul de schimb pentru determinarea echivalentului în euro este cel de la închiderea exerciţiului financiar precedent."* (pct. 13 verified in the 2014/2015 consolidated norms; its 2013 wording not separately confirmed.)
- 2014–2015 (**art. 112^6**, OUG 102/2013): *"Dacă în cursul unui an fiscal o microîntreprindere realizează venituri mai mari de 65.000 euro sau ponderea veniturilor realizate din consultanţă şi management în veniturile totale este de peste 20% inclusiv, aceasta va plăti impozit pe profit. Calculul şi plata impozitului pe profit se efectuează începând cu trimestrul în care s-a depăşit oricare dintre limitele prevăzute în prezentul articol, luând în considerare veniturile şi cheltuielile realizate de la începutul anului fiscal."* Rate: Norme pct. 13 as above.

Practical implication for the paper: for any calendar year t, both the status test on year-(t−1) revenue and the in-year exit test in year t use BNR EUR/RON at 31 Dec t−1, so a single lei threshold per year suffices, e.g. 60,000 EUR = 298,446 lei for 2025 (ANAF Cluj guide, implying 4.9741 at 31.12.2024); 500,000 EUR ≈ 2,473,700 lei for 2023 at 4.9474 (rate **[secondary]**); 100,000 EUR ≈ 509,850 lei for 2026 at 5.0985 **[secondary]**. ## 4. Checks against the draft's assumptions

1. **"Thresholds applying to revenue realised in year t: 2014: 65k, 2015: 65k, 2016: 100k, 2017: 500k (from Feb 2017), 2018–2022: 1M, 2023–2024: 500k, 2025: 250k, 2026: 100k."** Correct as the **in-year exit limit** for every year (for 2017 the 500k limit was in force from 6 Jan 2017 and the status switch from 1 Feb 2017; for 2016 note the status test at 31 Dec 2015 was 100k, but the in-year exit limit for 2015 revenue was 65k). It is **not** always the ceiling against which year-t revenue was tested for year-(t+1) status:
   - **2022 revenue** was tested at 31 Dec 2022 against **1,000,000 EUR**, not 500,000 (Legea 370/2022, Note A). A 500k bunching test on end-2022 revenue is therefore mis-specified; the first 31-Dec test at 500k is on **2023** revenue.
   - **2024 revenue** was tested at 31 Dec 2024 against **250,000 EUR** (OUG 156/2024 art. 54 (3)), even though the 2024 in-year exit limit was 500,000. Likewise **2025 revenue** was tested against **100,000 EUR** for 2026 status while the 2025 in-year limit was 250,000. If the analysis is about status-preserving bunching at year end, the series should be: 2013: 65k; 2014: 65k; 2015: 100k (status 2016); 2016: 500k (status from Feb 2017; 100k for Jan 2017); 2017–2021: 1M; 2022: 1M; 2023: 500k; 2024: 250k; 2025: 100k; 2026: 100k.
   - OUG 156/2024 was published on 31 Dec 2024, so the 250k test on 2024 revenue left essentially no anticipation window in the statute (the draft ordinance circulated in December 2024 **[secondary]**).
2. **"1%/3% rate boundary at 60k EUR revenue in 2023–2025."** Wrong for 2023: the 2023 rate was a flat 1% (OG 16/2022 art. 51 (1)). The 60k boundary exists only for **2024 and 2025** (Legea 296/2023), and firms with any listed CAEN activity (IT 5821/5829/6201/6209 (+6210/6290 from 2025), HoReCa 5510–5630 (+5611/5612/5622), law firms 6910, medical 8621–8690) paid 3% regardless of revenue, so the 60k notch does not apply to them. From 2026 the rate is 1% flat again (OUG 89/2025). Note the 60k boundary is a rate step within the regime (tax on all revenue jumps from 1% to 3% from the quarter of crossing), not an exit.
3. **"One-employee requirement from 1 January 2023 with hiring needed by 31 December 2022 to keep status."** Correct. Art. 47 (1) lit. g) is tested "la data de 31 decembrie a anului fiscal precedent"; ANAF Brașov guide (01.11.2022) confirms the 31 Dec 2022 test and gives examples of employees held at that date. There is no statutory grace period for existing firms (only 30 days for new firms, art. 48 (3), and 30 days to replace a departing sole employee, art. 52 (3)). "Employee" includes part-time contracts summing to a full norm and administration/mandate contracts paid at least the minimum wage (art. 51 (4)) — so the requirement could be met without a full-time hire.
4. **"OG 16/2022 (July 2022) setting 2023 status by 31-Dec-2022 revenue."** Partly wrong. OG 16/2022 (MO 716/15.07.2022) did set the 2023 rules, but Legea 370/2022 (MO 1228/20.12.2022) deferred the 500k revenue ceiling to 2023 revenue; only the non-revenue conditions were decided by 31 Dec 2022 data. See Note A and item 1.
5. **"OUG 156/2024 (December 2024) setting 250k for 2025 and 100k for 2026."** Correct: OUG 156/2024, MO 1334/31.12.2024, art. LXIV pct. 2 and 6, in force 1 Jan 2025; 250k tested on 31 Dec 2024 revenue and 100k on 31 Dec 2025 revenue. Two caveats: (i) the same act abrogated the consulting/management cap and extended the 3% CAEN list; (ii) OUG 8/2026 (MO 147/25.02.2026) re-based the 100k test on accounting turnover (cifra de afaceri) including linked enterprises, applicable to the 2026 status test, so the 2026 "revenue" variable differs from the 2013–2025 taxable-base definition.
6. **"In-year test converting at the BNR rate at the close of the previous fiscal year."** Correct for all years (Norme pct. 13 for 2013–2015; art. 52 (2) for 2016–2022; art. 52 (5) from 2023). The 31-Dec status test uses the close of the year in which the revenue was recorded, which is the same date.
7. **Further points the draft should reflect.** (a) The regime was mandatory 2013–2022 (with capital-based opt-outs: 25,000 EUR new firms 2013–2016; 45,000 lei 2017; 45,000 lei + 2 employees Apr 2018–2022) and optional from 2023, which changes the interpretation of bunching from below vs. above the threshold. (b) In 2016 the rate depended on employee count (1/2/3%), in 2017–2022 on having ≥ 1 employee (1/3%). (c) The consulting/management 20% cap applied 2014–2017 and 2023–2024 only. (d) The 25% holding rule allowed three micros in 2023 and one from 2024, with revenue aggregated over linked enterprises from 2024. (e) VAT: 220,000 lei through 31 Mar 2018, 300,000 lei from 1 Apr 2018, 395,000 lei from 1 Sep 2025 (OG 22/2025, not Legea 141/2025).
